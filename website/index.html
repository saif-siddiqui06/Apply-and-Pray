Everything a job seeker does all day

Each feature unlocks as you finish a short setup step, so you only set up what you use.

⚡

Easy Apply

Searches your job titles, skips jobs that ask for too much experience, and fills the quick-apply forms, answering questions from your profile.

🏢

Company websites

Fills application forms on company career sites (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Google Forms and plain forms), and creates accounts where needed.

📰

Jobs from the feed

Many jobs are only posted as “We're hiring, send your CV to…”. It finds them, fills linked forms, and writes the email with your resume attached.

🤝

Connection requests

Reaches the person who posted each job you applied to, plus recruiters and alumni from your college, with a short personal note.

💬

Personal messages

When someone accepts, it writes a short message about the role you applied for. One polite follow-up, and it stops the moment they reply.

✨

AI that knows your resume

Answers new form questions, writes notes and emails from your resume. Works with Google Gemini's free plan, OpenAI, DeepSeek or a model on your PC.

How it works

About 15 minutes to set up. A dashboard in your browser walks you through it.

Download & open

Unzip the app and double-click ApplyAndPray.exe. Your dashboard opens in your browser.

Set up, step by step

Your basics and resume, then log in to LinkedIn in the app's own browser window. Add an AI key and Gmail if you want those features.

Run it

Pick a mode: Easy Apply, company websites, feed jobs or networking. Watch it work, pause or stop it any time.

Review & reply

Approve the emails, requests and messages it wrote. When someone replies, it's your turn: the People tab shows who.

Built to be careful

  • Runs on your computer. No account, no server: your details, history and resume stay on your PC.
  • Passwords and keys are locked away in Windows Credential Manager, never in a plain file.
  • You approve before anything is sent: every email, connection request and message waits for you.
  • Slow and limited by default: daily and weekly limits, working hours only, and random pauses like a person.
  • Stops at the first warning about invitation limits, and never messages someone twice for the same thing.
  • Open source (AGPL-3.0): anyone can read exactly what it does.

Please read before using. Automating LinkedIn is against LinkedIn's User Agreement. Accounts that do too much, too fast, can be restricted. The app's defaults are deliberately cautious, but the risk is never zero, and raising the limits raises it. You use it at your own risk.

Quick start

  1. Unzip the download: right-click it → Extract All → choose a folder (Eg: Documents). Keep all its files together.
  2. Open the folder and double-click ApplyAndPray.exe. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway (why).
  3. Your dashboard opens in the browser. In Setup, fill Your basics and upload your resume (PDF).
  4. LinkedIn login: click Open LinkedIn and log in once in the window that opens. The app never sees your password.
  5. AI assistant: paste your Gemini key and click Save and test. Add keys from other Google accounts as backups for long runs.
  6. Go to Run, pick Easy Apply, tick Test run the first time (it fills forms but sends nothing), and click Start.

Every step has a detailed guide: read the setup guides.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is free and open source. The AI part can use Google Gemini's free plan; paid AI providers are optional.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Download, open, and follow the setup steps in the dashboard. Each step has a short guide.

Will my LinkedIn account get banned?

It can happen with any automation, which is why the app is slow and limited by default, asks you before sending messages, and stops at LinkedIn's first warning. Keep the default limits, don't run it all day, and stop if LinkedIn warns you.

Does it see my LinkedIn password?

No. You log in yourself, once, in the app's own browser window, and it stays logged in there. The app never asks for or stores your LinkedIn password.

What does it send without asking me?

Job applications (that's the point). Emails, connection requests and messages wait for your approval in the Review tab, unless you choose to turn reviewing off.

Where is my data?

On your PC only, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ApplyAndPray. Passwords and keys are in Windows Credential Manager. Uninstalling the app keeps your data; delete that folder to remove it.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

The Windows app is Windows-only. From source it runs anywhere Python 3.11 and Chrome do, but it's tested most on Windows.
