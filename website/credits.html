What this version adds

  • Applying on company websites, saved answers and the control window
  • The dashboard: step-by-step setup with guides, Run, Review, People and Applications
  • Jobs posted in the LinkedIn feed, by form or by email
  • Networking: connection requests, personal messages, follow-ups, recruiter and alumni search
  • Privacy and the Windows app: settings outside the code, keys in Windows Credential Manager
