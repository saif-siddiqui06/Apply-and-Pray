1. Install

About 2 minutes. You need Windows 10 or 11 and Google Chrome.

  1. Install or update Google Chrome if you don't have it.
  2. Download ApplyAndPray-windows.zip (about 45 MB).
  3. Right-click it → Extract All, choose a folder (Eg: Documents). Keep all the files together.
  4. Open ApplyAndPray.exe. A small black window opens (keep it open while you use the app) and your dashboard opens in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:5000.

Closing the small window stops the app. Opening the app again while it runs just shows the dashboard.

“Windows protected your PC”

Windows shows this for apps that aren't signed with a paid certificate, which this free project doesn't have yet.

  1. Click More info.
  2. Check the publisher line says “Unknown publisher” and the file is ApplyAndPray.exe from the official download page.
  3. Click Run anyway. Windows remembers your choice.

The code is open: anyone can read it, or build the app themselves with packaging\build.ps1.

2. Your basics

Used in every application form, and to search jobs for you. Unlocks nothing alone, but everything needs it.

  1. In the dashboard, open Setup → Your basics.
  2. Fill your name, application email, phone (without the country code) and location.
  3. Upload your resume as a PDF. The dashboard shows the text it could read from it: if it's empty, your PDF is a scan or an image, and job websites can't read it either. Export it as a text PDF from Word / Google Docs.
  4. Add the job titles you want (one per line), where, and your years of experience (0 if you're a fresher). Jobs asking for more experience are skipped.
  5. Click Save.

3. LinkedIn login

The bot works inside its own Chrome window, logged in to your LinkedIn. Unlocks Easy Apply, company websites and feed jobs.

  1. Close any Chrome window the bot opened before.
  2. In Setup → LinkedIn login, click Open LinkedIn. A separate Chrome window opens: the bot's own browser, not your normal Chrome.
  3. Log in as usual, including the code LinkedIn may send you.
  4. When your feed shows, the step turns green by itself and the window closes.

You stay logged in for future runs. The app never asks for or saves your LinkedIn password.

4. AI key (Google Gemini, free)

Answers new questions in forms, and writes notes, emails and messages from your resume. Optional, but recommended.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and copy it.
  3. In Setup → AI assistant, choose Google Gemini, paste the key and click Save and test.
  4. Backup AI (recommended, free): in the same step choose Groq (key at console.groq.com/keys) or OpenRouter (openrouter.ai/keys). It answers automatically whenever Gemini can't.
  5. Backup keys (recommended for long runs): make another key the same way with a different Google account, and paste it in Backup Gemini keys (several keys: separate them with commas). When one key's free daily limit is used up, the app switches to the next.

The free plan has a daily limit. When a model's limit is used up, the app switches to another model, and when all are used up it continues without AI until the next day (questions it can't answer are asked in a popup, or the job is saved for you).

Other options: OpenAI (paid: key at platform.openai.com/api-keys, model gpt-4o-mini), DeepSeek (paid, low cost: platform.deepseek.com/api_keys, model deepseek-chat), or a free model on your own PC with Ollama / LM Studio (choose “Local model”).

Your key is kept in Windows Credential Manager, never in a file.

5. Application details

Common questions on company websites, and an account for job sites that need one. Unlocks company-website applications.

  1. In Setup → Application details, fill your expected salary, notice period, LinkedIn link and so on.
  2. Job-site password: many career sites need an account; the app creates and signs in to them with this email and password. Use a new password you don't use anywhere else (not your Gmail or LinkedIn password), with 8+ characters, a capital letter, a number and a symbol. Leave it empty and those jobs are saved for you to apply by hand.

6. Gmail app password

For jobs posted as “send your CV to hr@company.com”: the app emails your resume from your Gmail, after you approve each email.

  1. Open myaccount.google.com/security and turn on 2-Step Verification (Google requires it).
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Type a name, Eg: Apply and Pray, and click Create.
  4. Copy the 16-letter password (spaces don't matter).
  5. In Setup → Gmail, enter your Gmail address and that password, then Save and test: you'll get a test email from yourself.

This is not your Gmail password, and you can delete it on the same Google page any time to cut the app off. School and work Google accounts may not allow app passwords.

7. Networking

Connection requests to people who can help, and personal messages after they accept. Unlocks the Networking run.

  1. In Setup → Networking, write your pitch in 2-3 lines: what you study or do, your top skills, the roles you want, one result you're proud of. Every note and message is written from it.
  2. Tick whether you have LinkedIn Premium (free accounts can add a note to only a few invitations a month), and add your college to reach alumni.
  3. Keep the limits as they are unless you know what you're doing.
  4. In Review, click Find people to connect with, read the requests, edit or skip any, then approve.
  5. Go to Run → Networking. Run it again on later days: it checks who accepted, writes their messages (you approve), sends one follow-up after 6 days without a reply, and never writes again once they reply.

Running it well

  • New? Tick Test run on the Run tab first: the bot fills every form but never presses the final Submit, so you can watch it safely.
  • Run during the day, for an hour or two, not all day long.
  • Keep the default limits. If LinkedIn ever shows a warning, stop for a few days.
  • Check Review once a day: approve what looks right, edit what doesn't.
  • Check Questions: questions the bot couldn't answer wait there. Answer them once ("Save for all jobs") and the bot uses the answers from then on.
  • If something stops working, click Run → Check LinkedIn: it reads every kind of LinkedIn page the bot uses and tells you which part changed.
  • The defaults are safe: at most 30 applications a day, pauses between them, a short break every 10. Raising them raises the risk to your account.
  • Check People for “Replied: your turn”, and answer them yourself.
  • Open Applications → Apply by hand for jobs the app couldn't finish.

Troubleshooting

Chrome doesn't open, or closes at once

Update Google Chrome, close all Chrome windows the bot opened, and try again. The first run downloads a small Chrome helper, so it needs internet.

The LinkedIn step doesn't turn green

Make sure you got all the way to your LinkedIn feed in the window the app opened (not your normal Chrome). It waits up to 5 minutes; click Open LinkedIn again if needed.

“AI isn't working” or the free limit is used up

The free Gemini plan resets every day. Click Test again tomorrow, or add a paid provider. The app keeps working without AI.

Gmail test fails

Use an app password (16 letters), not your normal password, and check 2-Step Verification is on. Make a new app password if unsure.

Where are the logs?

In %LOCALAPPDATA%\ApplyAndPray\logs\log.txt. When a page can't be read, a copy is saved in logs\easy_apply_forms: attach it if you report a problem.
