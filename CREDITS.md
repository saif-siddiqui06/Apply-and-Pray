## This version adds

- Normal Apply on company websites, saved answers and the "missing detail" popup, the control window
- The local dashboard: step-by-step setup with guides and checks, Run controls, Review queue, People and Applications pages
- Feed jobs (applying to jobs posted as LinkedIn posts, by form or by email)
- Networking: connection requests with notes, personal messages, follow-ups, recruiter and alumni search
- Privacy and publishing: settings outside the code, keys in Windows Credential Manager, SQLite history, the Windows app and the website
